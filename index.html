About the School

A Centre of Learning in North Gondar

Adiarkay Secondary and Preparatory School stands as one of the leading educational institutions in the Adiarkay area of North Gondar Zone. We are committed to providing quality education that prepares students for national examinations and university entrance.

Our school nurtures intellectual curiosity, discipline, and character — values that carry students well beyond the classroom and into productive, meaningful lives.

Our Mission

"To provide every student with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to excel in university education and contribute meaningfully to the development of Ethiopia."

Our Vision

"To be the leading preparatory school in North Gondar, recognized for academic achievement, ethical leadership, and community service."

Admissions

Join Adiarkay School

We welcome students who are committed to academic excellence and personal growth. Admission is open to students completing Grade 8 (for secondary) and Grade 10 (for preparatory).

1
Check Eligibility
Completed Grade 8 with passing marks for secondary admission. Grade 10 national exam results required for preparatory.
→
2
Collect Documents
Exam results, birth certificate, previous school ID card, and two passport-size photographs.
→
3
Visit the School
Come to the school registrar's office during working hours (Monday–Friday, 8AM–5PM).
→
4
Complete Registration
Submit documents, pay registration fee, and receive your student ID and timetable.
📅
Registration Period: Registration opens following the release of national examination results each year. Contact the school for the current academic year's specific dates.
